Privacy Policy for Nox Board

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Welcome to Nox Board! This privacy policy outlines how we handle information in our keyboard application. Our core philosophy is absolute privacy.

1. Introduction

Noven built the Nox Board app as an Open Source app. This SERVICE is provided by Noven at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

2. Zero Data Collection (100% Offline)

Nox Board is a completely offline, privacy-first keyboard. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your keystrokes, personal data, passwords, or typing behavior.

The app does not even request Internet permission, making it technically impossible for us or any third party to access your data remotely.

3. Local Storage Only

Any data generated by the app (such as your personal dictionary for word prediction, custom themes, and keyboard layouts) is stored locally and securely on your device. This data never leaves your phone and is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Permissions Required

To function properly as a customizable keyboard, Nox Board requires the following minimal permissions:

  • Vibration Control: Required to provide haptic feedback (vibration) when you press a key.
  • Read/Write Storage (Optional): Only requested if you choose to import/export custom themes or set a custom background image for your keyboard.

5. Open Source Transparency

Nox Board is fully open-source. The entire source code is available on GitHub for anyone to inspect and verify our privacy claims. We believe that true privacy requires transparency.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about my Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact me at noxtan.dev@proton.me.
