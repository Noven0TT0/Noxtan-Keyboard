Privacy Policy for Nox Board

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Welcome to Nox Board! This privacy policy outlines how we handle information in our keyboard application. Our core philosophy is absolute privacy and complete transparency.

1. Introduction

Noven built the Nox Board app under the brand NoxTan as an Open Source, free-of-charge application. This SERVICE is provided by Noven at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page informs users regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

2. Zero Data Collection (100% Offline)

Nox Board is a completely offline, privacy-first keyboard. We do not collect, store, transmit, track, or share any of your keystrokes, personal data, passwords, or typing behavior.

The app does not request Android Internet permission, making it technically impossible for us or any third party to access or transmit your data remotely.

3. Local Storage & Word Learning

Any data generated while using the keyboard is stored locally and securely on your device only:

  • Personal Dictionary: Words learned to improve typing suggestions remain in a local database. You can clear this data at any time via Settings > Manage Dictionary > "Clear Learned Words", or by enabling Incognito Mode.
  • Custom Themes & Layouts: Custom settings are saved solely within your device's private app storage.

This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the application.

4. Clipboard Data

Nox Board includes an optional on-device clipboard history feature to help you quickly paste recently copied items:

  • Clipboard contents are processed and retained solely in local device memory and storage for your personal convenience.
  • Clipboard data is never uploaded, tracked, or shared with anyone.
  • You can manage, unpin, or permanently delete clipboard history items directly from the clipboard panel at any time.

5. Permissions Required

To function properly as a customizable keyboard while respecting your privacy, Nox Board requires only minimal device access:

  • Vibration Control (VIBRATE): Required solely to provide haptic feedback (vibration) when you press a key.
  • Wallpaper & Theme Files (Optional): When you choose to set a custom background image or import/export a theme, Nox Board uses the standard Android System File Picker. We do not request broad or dangerous storage access permissions.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13, nor do we collect personal data from any user regardless of age.

7. Open Source Transparency

Nox Board is fully open-source. The entire source code is available on GitHub for anyone to inspect, audit, and verify our zero-data-collection claims. We believe true privacy requires absolute transparency.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the application does not have internet access, any changes will be reflected directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact me at noxtan.dev@proton.me.
